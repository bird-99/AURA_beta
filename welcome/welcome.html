Welcome to AURA

AURA suggests gentle accessibility improvements that stay on your device. You decide when to enable, pause, or never use them.

Open Settings See how it works

What you get

Local-only intelligence

Signals are processed in your browser. AURA never uploads pages or browsing data.

User agency

Each suggestion stays until you choose: Enable, Not now, or Never. Restore is one click away.

Site access, explained

Host access lets AURA apply the fixes you approve on any site you visit—nothing else.

Quick setup

  1. Open Settings and choose your default modes for Comfort Visual and Focus.
  2. Try on a safe site like Wikipedia, then accept or dismiss the first suggestions.
  3. Manage per-site rules in Domains (Allow / Never) and restore anytime from the banner.

Trust & permissions

Why does AURA need site access?

So it can apply readability and focus tweaks on the pages you visit, only when you approve.

What data is stored?

Local preferences and per-site decisions. Everything stays on your device.

How do I revoke access?

Open Settings → Domains and update Allow/Never rules at any time.

Privacy summary

We do

  • Use local signals to suggest accessibility changes.
  • Store preferences and per-site keys on your device.
  • Apply only what you choose, with undo built in.

We don’t

  • Upload pages or browsing history.
  • Sell data or track identity.
  • Change content without your approval.

You can always reopen Settings from the extension menu.
