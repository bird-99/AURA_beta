Privacy-first accessibility

Welcome to AURA

AURA suggests gentle accessibility improvements that stay on your device. You stay in control.

Get Started

Local-only intelligence

All analysis happens in your browser. AURA never uploads browsing data—your habits and pages remain private.

User agency by design

AURA suggests, never forces. Banners persist until you choose: Enable, Not now, or Never. Restore is always available.

Why broad access?

Host access lets AURA apply accessibility fixes on the sites you visit. It is used solely for on-page adjustments you approve.

How it works

  1. Detects signals like zooming to understand when you need support.
  2. Suggests readability or focus tweaks without changing content.
  3. Lets you decide, with a one-click restore if you change your mind.

Need to adjust settings later? Open the AURA options from the extension menu.
